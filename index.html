Build on Saint Lucia’s systems

We propose working with government and GGGI to confirm where approved tags, ledger records and evidence of project results can be carried into the next reporting cycle.

Ministerial decision brief

FISH-ADAPT has US$16.71 million in approved financing and a published first GCF disbursement of US$3.16 million. FAO’s 2025 report describes inception progress. Decision requested: designate Finance, Fisheries and Sustainable Development counterparts to agree a project evidence exchange with FAO and review one reporting cycle.

FAO and GCF draft APR brief

2025 inception comprised steering committee formation, two meetings and an inception workshop. The project’s published expected reach is approximately 72,000 people. Period actuals need approved records. Confirm the current APR review status and any GCF feedback with FAO.

Finance and audit reconciliation

Approved GCF grant US$14,750,000; cash co-financing grant US$1,656,712; in-kind US$307,250. Published first GCF disbursement US$3,158,997, or 21.42% of the GCF grant. Match the receipt, ledger, supporting vouchers and period before reporting actual expenditure or utilisation. Record unresolved exceptions and their disposition.

Fisheries and M&E results brief

Track the four approved outcome areas: safer fishing operations, ecosystem health and sustainable practices, resilient and diversified livelihoods, and stronger institutions. Candidate evidence includes vessel safety records, field observations, livelihoods monitoring and institutional records. Confirm indicator IDs, units, baselines, targets and actuals against the approved register before use.

Vulnerability, safeguards and gender brief

Relevant hazards include storms, sea-level rise, ocean warming and acidification. Inland aquaculture also faces heat and changing precipitation. GCF lists Category C. Review the approved gender plan, consultation and applicable safeguards records. Current action completion and disaggregated actuals await project evidence.

National MRV exchange and public disclosure

Proposed fields are country LC, project SAP053, reporting period, event or indicator ID, financing stage, amount and currency or unit, source, evidence status and approval date. Release approved totals and summaries. Record the approximately 72,000 figure as expected reach.

Next disbursement evidence brief

Open items are the governing agreement conditions, updated tranche instructions, signed financial reconciliation, forward cash needs and current results and compliance evidence. FAO confirms eligibility and authorises the official request.
