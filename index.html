Saint Lucia · Proposal for technical discussion

Climate finance and project results

A pilot to connect government financial records and verified project results to Saint Lucia’s existing Climate Finance Tracking System and MRV portal.

Review the proposed pilot

Build on Saint Lucia’s systems

Saint Lucia already tracks climate finance flows and links them to climate actions. CLIMB-SLU also covers operationalising climate and gender tagging in government budgeting. We propose working with government and GGGI to confirm where approved tags, ledger records and evidence of project results can be carried into the next reporting cycle.

AreaWhat to confirmProposed pilot output
Finance reconciliationHow commitments and disbursements tie to receipts, ledger expenditure and direct payments.Source-linked reconciliation with control totals and a reviewed exception register.
Project resultsHow financial records map to activities and how reported results tie to field evidence.An indicator register with definitions, targets, observations and reviewer decisions.
ReportingHow approved climate and gender tags and verified records flow into national and funder reports.One reporting pack and one controlled data exchange file using government-approved mappings.

The assessment will confirm existing back-office capabilities, funded work and the status of the CLIMB-SLU rollout.

FISH-ADAPT · SAP053

A public-data demonstration for a Saint Lucian fisheries and aquaculture adaptation project. FAO is the GCF Accredited Entity.

Financing and reconciliation

The US$14,750,000 GCF grant, US$1,656,712 co-financing grant and US$307,250 in-kind contribution sum to US$16,713,962. The first disbursement was US$3,158,997. Expenditure and realised co-financing require source records and approval.

Results, milestones and safeguards

FAO’s 2025 report describes the inception workshop, steering committee and baseline survey procurement. The GCF portfolio lists the project as Category C. The pilot would verify indicator actuals, gender action progress and supporting records with authorised project staff.

What the GCFT test showed

The Saint Lucia workspace accepted the demonstration record and confirmed saving the six-section APR draft. Project-specific Reports Hub routing and scheduled report generation remain to be validated. The test used public records. FAO’s official 2025 report is under GCF review.

From assessment to pilot

Four-week assessment

Walk through one reporting cycle, confirm system owners and CLIMB-SLU scope, measure current effort and agree a costed pilot.

12-week pilot

Subject to assessment, use one agreed project, one approved finance extract and one results register for a closed period.

Government acceptance

Finance signs off the reconciliation. Sustainable Development reviews the evidence mapping. The project owner approves the reporting pack.

Requested decision. Convene a scoping session with the GCF National Designated Authority, Finance, Sustainable Development and GGGI. Discuss FISH-ADAPT as a candidate with the project team and FAO.
